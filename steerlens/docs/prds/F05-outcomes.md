# PRD F05 — Outcomes

**Slice:** 1 · **Mockup:** `steerlens-exec-04-outcomes.png`

## Problem

Leaders need the headline outcome narrative, not a metrics warehouse.

## Goal

Present one primary outcome with large numbers and funded bets as quiet rows.

## User stories

- As a CTO, I see whether the outcome is being achieved in one glance.
- As a Director, I can jump to a bet row for details.

## Requirements

1. Primary outcome hero metric(s) from SteerSpec
2. Short interpretation sentence (from metric.interpretation or generated summary)
3. Bet rows with simple progress cue
4. Manual edit of metric current/target in Slice 1 (inline or secondary form)

## Acceptance

- Sample shows delivery rate hero and three bet rows
- Editing a metric updates SteerSpec

## XFN

A11y: numeric text alternatives · No colour-only trend

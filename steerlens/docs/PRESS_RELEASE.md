# Press release — SteerLens

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE** (draft)

## SteerLens brings investment clarity to engineering leadership — without another planning silo

**London, UK** — Today, Matthew Worthington introduces **SteerLens**, an interactive briefing space for Engineering Directors and executive sponsors who need a clear answer to three questions: *Are we funding the right outcomes? Is the organisation shaped to deliver them? What should we stop?*

Most enterprises already have Jira for work, Backstage for services, and Entra or GitHub for people and teams. None of those tools own the **investment contract** — the living link between business outcomes, funded bets, team topology intent, and the evidence that should trigger a stop or re-scope. Leaders end up stitching slide decks by hand. Alignment decays between steering committees.

SteerLens treats that contract as a first-class artifact. Leaders open a local workspace, shape a plain-language map of outcomes and bets, organise how work is delivered across customer teams and shared platforms, and leave with a **decision note** fit for a board pack — not a backlog.

### Not another system of record

SteerLens does not replace planning tools or directories. When organisations later connect Backstage, GitHub, or Entra, SteerLens **references** teams that already exist. It never invents competing catalog Groups for provider-synced estates. Technical exports and overlays remain optional; the default experience stays executive-safe.

### Built beside ArchLens — deliberately different

SteerLens sits in the same product family as **ArchLens**, the living systems-architecture suite. The relationship is intentional and narrow: a funded bet may later point at architecture risk in ArchLens. The experiences do **not** share a visual language. ArchLens is a builder’s tool. SteerLens is a steering-committee surface — light, typographic, and free of YAML in the default views.

### Local-first by design

The first release works entirely on the user’s machine: no account, no cloud sync required. Leaders can trial the model on a real transformation narrative before connecting any system. Authentication and live integrations follow once the SteerSpec contract proves useful in the room where decisions are made.

### Availability

Product specification, mockups, and architecture are published in draft under the author’s open portfolio. A dedicated open-source repository and Slice 1 implementation will follow.
